* SPECIFICATION
  - =SOURCE/Main.cpp= opens one desktop window of 400 by 300 pixels titled =WindowTriangle=
  - the window shows one white filled triangle on a black background
  - =calculate_triangle_points(width, height)= in =SOURCE/TrianglePoints.cpp= returns the three corners
    - top: half the width across and 20 down
    - bottom left: 20 across and 20 up from the bottom
    - bottom right: 20 in from the right and 20 up from the bottom
  - =TriangleComponent::paint()= in =SOURCE/TriangleComponent.cpp= fills a =juce::Path= through those corners
  - closing the window ends the program

* DECISIONS
  - this drill builds with CMake against =SUBMODULES/JUCE=. =make= wraps the two =cmake= commands
  - =SOURCE/TrianglePoints.cpp= includes no JUCE header. The Catch2 test builds with =g++= alone
  - the window itself is checked by eye
