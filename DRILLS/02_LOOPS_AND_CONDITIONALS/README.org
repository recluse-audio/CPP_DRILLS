* SPECIFICATION
  Three programs. Each prints to standard output and exits 0.
  - =SOURCE/triangle.cpp= prints seven lines. Line n is n =#= characters
  - =SOURCE/fizz_buzz.cpp= prints the numbers 1 to 100 one per line
    - =Fizz= replaces a multiple of 3
    - =Buzz= replaces a multiple of 5
    - =FizzBuzz= replaces a multiple of both
  - =SOURCE/chessboard.cpp= prints 8 lines of 8 characters that alternate a space and =#=
    - line one starts on a space
    - each line starts on the opposite character from the line above
  - =SOURCE/LoopPrograms.cpp= has =write_triangle(output)= and =write_fizz_buzz(output)= and
    =write_chessboard(output, size)=. Each program calls one of them with =std::cout=
